# Pacific Fighter Sweep
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Windows PC, native Unity game.

## Stack
Unity 6 LTS, C#, URP, Unity Input System. Explicitly specified in prompt.md.

## Product Purpose
A playable P-51D versus four A6M Zero dogfight over the Pacific. Physics, energy management and pilot controls drive combat.

## Users
PC flight simulation players using keyboard, mouse, gamepad or flight controls.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Prioritize the first playable dogfight. Shared Rigidbody aerodynamics, finite ballistic ammunition, component damage, deterministic AI and clear flight instruments. 60 FPS is a target requiring measurement, not an established claim.

## Brand Commitments
WWII reflector gunsight; unobtrusive HUD. Pacific ocean, islands, clouds and atmospheric haze. Specification authorizes clean placeholder artwork.

## Evidence on Hand
prompt.md only. No existing aircraft, textures, audio or Unity project at inspection.
