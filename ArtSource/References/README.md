# Aircraft reference notes

The Mustang reference is the scanned USAF T.O. 1F-51D-1 flight manual (formerly AN 01-60JE-1), downloaded from https://stephentaylorhistorian.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/p-51d-manual-b.pdf . Printed pages 1 and 3–6 show dimensions, general arrangement and cockpit controls. The model follows its 37-foot span and 32-foot 2-inch length, with a throttle quadrant, radio control box and oxygen regulator added from the cockpit views. This is a late-service manual, so fittings are reference-informed rather than a claim of a specific wartime serial's exact cockpit.

A6M2 overall span/length and radial-engine identification were cross-checked against the National Naval Aviation Museum's aircraft record: https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/museums/nnam/explore/collections/aircraft/a/a6m2-zero0.html . The Zero retains its framed canopy and receives paired gun breech housings and metric flight instruments. A complete A6M2 manufacturer drawing set has not been obtained, so blueprint-exact Zero fidelity remains unverified.

Geometry, subtle paint-wear textures and instrument faces are generated by this project; museum photographs/manual illustrations are references and are not embedded as aircraft textures.
