# WWII paint and nationality references

The user's `images/planes.png`, `images/bf109.png` and `images/p38.png` are the visual authorities for this livery pass. The profile sheet supplies the dark-green Zero Type 52 and yellow-nosed, invasion-striped P-51D; the two additional images now supply the Bf 109 and P-38 schemes, superseding their earlier representative paint choices. The game retains its A6M2 flight model and Bf 109 G-6/P-38J geometry: appearance references do not silently change aircraft variants. These notes record the requested appearance; final rendered verification of the latest livery changes is pending.

- **Mustang:** aluminum body, yellow nose/spinner, olive upper patches, black/white invasion stripes, E2/C and Lou IV-inspired lettering, WWII white star-and-bars with blue surround. The supplied European livery is intentionally retained in this sandbox Pacific scenario.
- **Zero:** dark-green upper paint, light gray-green underside, dark radial cowling, red Hinomaru on fuselage and upper/lower wings, pale 53-104 tail code from the reference.
- **Bf 109 (`images/bf109.png`):** mottled gray camouflage and pale underside, yellow rear fuselage band and lower cowling, black spinner, outlined 67 fuselage number, Balkenkreuz and historical tail marking. Follow this supplied photograph rather than borrowing the profile sheet's Fw 190 shape or unit number.
- **P-38 (`images/p38.png`):** silver/aluminum airframe, olive anti-glare panels on the pod and nacelles, red spinners, 426981 tail serial and 981 nose number. Retain WWII US star-and-bars on the upper-left/lower-right wings and outer boom sides; the supplied photograph guides finish and numbered markings rather than certifying a single wartime configuration.

Nation flags are displayed in the selector; aircraft use their service insignia, not rectangular flags pasted over the airframe. WWII US flags have 48 stars (six straight rows of eight); Japan uses the white/red Hinomaru; the German flag is the 1935–1945 red/white-disc/black-swastika design. These are historical identifiers for the WWII roster.
