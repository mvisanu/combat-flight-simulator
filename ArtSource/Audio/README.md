# Engine recording provenance

`MerlinCruise.wav` is an edited, filtered, normalized and crossfaded 7.5-second engine loop derived from John Veit's own recording of three P-51 Mustangs at Paine Field in June 2010.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:3_P-51_Mustangs_start-up_and_make_a_fly-by_at_Paine_Field_USA_June_2010_with_sound.ogv
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal, as stated by the author on the source page.
- Original retained here as `mustangs-paine-field-2010.ogv`.
- Reproducible edit: `Tools/author_engine_audio.py`, source time 00:30–00:38, 55 Hz high-pass / 3 kHz low-pass, mono 22,050 Hz, 0.5-second seam crossfade.

This field recording is used for the Mustang only. The Zero, Bf 109 and P-38 retain synthesized fallback engine audio rather than mislabeling the Mustang recording as their engines. Source identity and sample validity are verified; a human headphone audition remains useful for mix quality.
